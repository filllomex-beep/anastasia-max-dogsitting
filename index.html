About us

What matters most to usis that every dog feels calm, comfortable and cared for while their owner is away.

Hi! We're Nastya and Max, a couple who adore animals of every breed and personality. We've cared for tiny pups and big breeds like the Belgian Shepherd, and we look after every pet as if it were our own, making sure they feel safe, happy and at home.

We both work from home in IT and business, so we're around most of the day, with plenty of time for play, walks and quiet cuddles.

Before every stay, we meet with you to get to know each other and ask for your instructions, preferences and any little nuances, so that your time away causes as little disruption as possible for your four-legged child.

We're based an hour south of Barcelona, Spain, but we can drive to Barcelona or nearby cities to pick up and drop off your dog.

  1. We work from home. Your dog gets company, attention and cuddles all day.
  2. Three walks a day: morning, midday and evening.
  3. A dog-friendly flat with a closed balcony.
  4. Dogs are welcome on the sofa. Comfort is part of the stay.
  5. The dog beach is close by, and there's a small park right in front of our building.
  6. Experience with senior dogs, puppies and dogs that aren't keen on other dogs.

Is it for you

If this sounds like you, we should meet:

  • You need a sitter for a weekend, a week or a month
  • Your dog gets anxious when left alone. Don't worry, there are two of us, so we can accommodate this and make sure your dog is never left home alone.
  • You want a trustworthy sitter who always keeps in touch while you're away
  • Your dog needs special care (medication, puppy care, etc.), we've got you.
How it works

How a stay goesfrom one night to several weeks

Before the stay

Every dog is different, so we always get to know each other first and talk through all the details. With the owner's agreement, we can also include mini training sessions, games and bonding work during the stay. This helps reduce stress, keeps the dog's usual routine going, and makes their stay not only comfortable but fun too.

During the stay

We're in touch with you every day, sending photo and video reports so you don't miss your family member too much.

At pick-up

You get a happy and relaxed dog, sometimes even with a new toy to take home.
